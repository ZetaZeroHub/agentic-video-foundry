FAQ

What people want to know before starting

I do not edit video. Can I still use it?

Yes. Start by describing the audience, platform, length, material, and result you want. The Skill turns that into working documents and visible checkpoints you can approve.

Can I bring my own images, recordings, or existing video?

Yes. Real product footage and user-provided material can become the main evidence instead of being covered by decorative animation.

Can I change a voice or visual style I dislike?

Yes. You can audition another voice, rebalance the music, change sound effects, switch templates, or add a custom style before the final render.

Can I edit the result later?

Yes. Delivery can include the complete film, standalone chapter videos, narration, music, effects, captions, and the project files used to compose them.

Which AI tools can load it?

Codex, Claude Code, Trae, Gemini CLI, Kimi Code, MiniMax, WorkBuddy, Manus, and other environments that can load Agent Skills or repository instructions.

Do ElevenLabs or Volcengine cost extra?

The Skill is open source. Audio-provider usage follows the account and plan you connect, so preview short samples before approving paid generation.
