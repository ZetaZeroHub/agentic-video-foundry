THE WORKFLOW

Not a pile of effects.
A chain of evidence.

Every capability maps to a real action and a real deliverable. Motion explains, crops, and focuses; it never replaces the product evidence that should be on screen.

01

BRIEF

Diagnose the job

Lock the audience, platform, duration, message, CTA, and evidence the viewer must see.

  • One-line goal
  • Evidence plan
  • Explicit exclusions
02

DESIGN

Storyboard and style

Give every shot one job, then choose paper collage, pop art, or a real product demonstration.

  • One hero per shot
  • Style preset
  • Representative frames
03

AUDIO

Generate, audition, align

Route ElevenLabs or Volcengine across narration, voice candidates, AI music, and interactive SFX. Real audio timestamps drive captions and scenes.

  • Multi-voice audition
  • AI BGM + interaction SFX
  • Strict audio-caption alignment
04

ROUTING

Use each tool for its strength

Remotion owns the master timeline. Lottie and HyperFrames serve only the scenes they fit.

  • One master chain
  • Frozen exchange assets
  • Final revalidation
05

RENDER

Deterministic composition

Frame-driven motion keeps captions, sound cues, and batch variants reproducible.

  • Data timeline
  • No random drift
  • Still-frame preflight
06

DELIVERY

Verify before shipping

Full watch-through, phone-speaker review, encoding, loudness, true peak, and manifests are hard gates.

  • Visual QA
  • Audio QA
  • Editable assets

STYLE PRESETS

Two presets.
Not one palette.

Each style preset controls material, palette, typography, motion grammar, density limits, and hard failures. Switch below to see the preset take over this section.

BOLD
Four-color print
SNAP!

Palette

Ink black, warm paper, electric yellow, bright cyan, hot red, cobalt blue. Max 3 high-saturation colors per frame.

Motion

Energetic snap. 6-frame base unit, 12% hero overshoot, 18-frame result holds. Panel slam, ink wipe, match cut transitions only.

Density

1 hero per frame, max 2 supporters, max 3 panels at peak. Hero occupies 40-68% of canvas.

Forbidden

No 3D flip, no liquid morph, no strobing, no continuous camera breathing, no unseeded jitter.

Usage

assets/style-presets/comic-pop-art-motion.json

Copy into project before implementation. Override palette and motion tokens only. Never override single hero, 3-color limit, evidence readability, or deterministic frame drive.

Torn
Stop-motion collage

Palette

Warm fiber paper, clay brown, grass green, sky blue, warm yellow. Low-saturation offset print feel.

Motion

Stop-motion holds. 2-6 frame irregular holds, 6px micro-sway, max 3° rotation, single settle overshoot. Paper wipe, hand-set slide, page push transitions.

Density

1 hero per frame, max 2 supporters, max 6 paper groups. Subject occupies 35-60% of canvas.

Forbidden

No continuous floating, no shape morphing, no unseeded random jitter, no simultaneous everything.

Usage

assets/style-presets/flat-paper-collage-stop-motion.json

Copy into project before implementation. Override palette, texture opacity, edge roughness, hold patterns only. Never override single hero, Chinese priority, rigid paper, deterministic motion, or evidence preservation.

FAQ

What teams ask before installing

Direct answers about compatibility, rendering, credentials, and what “done” means in this workflow.

What is Agentic Video Foundry?

It is an open-source Agent Skill that coordinates briefing, storyboarding, real demos, narration, music, sound effects, subtitles, rendering, visual review, audio measurement, and editable delivery.

Which coding agents can use it?

Current targets include Codex, Claude Code, Trae, Gemini CLI, Kimi Code, MiniMax, and other environments that can load Agent Skills or repository instructions.

Which renderer owns the timeline?

Remotion is the default master compositor. Text-to-Lottie and HyperFrames are optional asset or scene routes, not competing master timelines.

Are API keys or paid media public?

No. Credentials stay in the OS keychain or environment variables. Paid and private media remains outside Git history and enters only separately approved deployment packages.
